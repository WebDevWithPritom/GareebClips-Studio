## Why clippers use it
Most AI clippers work on a **finished** video, in **the cloud**, on a **monthly plan**. GareebClips Studio is built the other way round:

- **🔴 Live clipping:** paste a Twitch, Kick or YouTube channel link. While the streamer is live, clips are ready about a minute after each moment, so you can post first.
- **💻 Runs on your laptop:** Windows 10/11 and Mac (Apple Silicon). Works on 4 GB laptops, with a "Cool" mode that won't heat your machine. Fully offline except for the stream video itself.
- **🧠 Clips that make sense:** not just "loud = clip". Each clip is cut on full sentences as **setup → moment → payoff**. The app finds **what led up to it** (even minutes earlier) and the **aftermath**, and every clip gets a **"Why this clip"** score with reasons.
- **👥 Reads the room:** chat surges, laugh and hype emotes, and the streamer's facecam reactions count as hype signals. Twitch ad breaks are skipped automatically.
- **🎮 Made for gaming and campaigns:**
  - "Game + face" split layout, full-screen fit, and an action-tracking crop;
  - sentence-snapping visual timeline, split-at-playhead, and export presets (TikTok / Reels / Shorts / X);
  - offline clipper FX: GTA WASTED climax, screen shake, impact flash, and reaction badges (W, L, HYPE, RIP);
  - custom local B-roll and meme cutaways (drop in your own MP4/PNG memes);
  - punch zoom, best-of montage, and animated captions with a hook title;
  - campaign rules are checked before you export (required hashtag, streamer on screen, no logos);
  - clean-language mode masks swear words.
- **🇮🇳 Hindi and Hinglish:** speech in Hinglish, and a Hindi-aware meaning model.
- **📈 Gets smarter:** a day after each stream, the app compares its picks with what viewers actually clipped, and learns.
